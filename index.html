Auto Pulse

AI-Powered Vehicle Health, Diagnosis & Maintenance Intelligence Platform

Live Demo ↗

System Overview

Vehicle owners frequently struggle with fragmented service histories, unexpected repair invoices, and ambiguous mechanical symptoms. Auto Pulse resolves this friction by aggregating vehicle health diagnostics, maintenance planning, expense auditing, and garage discovery into a centralized digital management system.

Rather than relying on generic forms, the platform provides tailored vehicle profile management, derived health indexing, and structured symptom triage.

Core Capabilities

AI Vehicle Diagnosis

Analyzes natural language symptom descriptions to generate structured diagnoses with severity levels, probable mechanical causes, and estimated repair urgency.

Real-Time Telemetry Gauges

Monitors critical operational metrics including speed velocity, fuel reserve levels, and engine core operating temperatures.

Service & Cost Tracking

Monitors scheduled maintenance milestones, service recommendations, fuel logging, and categorizes monthly ownership expenses.

Nearby Garage Discovery

Location-assisted discovery of verified service centers and workshops with direct contact channels and verified specialties.
